# Pyramid rocket

## Design inspirations 

* delta clipper
* gravity force 2/thrust. 
* wipeout
* razorback (expanse)

## Design benefits

* match simple convex hull collision shape
* not getting stuck on geometry

## Downsides

* spring boom camera gets pushed inside ship when top pressed against surface. maybe could solve by adding a depression, or centre spring-boom on nose. 
* shading less than previous spaceship. maybe TODO copy the expanse razorback with light-up engine bay etc (mix of shrouding and open frame, like motorbikes)
