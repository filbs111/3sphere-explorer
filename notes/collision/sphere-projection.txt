collision detection of sphere - eg for player - with a AABB bvh object.

taking position of sphere in frame of object. currently assuming that object is small, so sphere is fixed size sphere in this frame.
this is an approximation.

consider 3d projected to 2D. here the "sphere" is a circle at position (x,y,z) with object centred at (0,0,1). currently using projected coords 
(x/z , y/z) , circle of radius r, so in the approximation, AABB (x/z -r , y/z - r) to (x/z +r , y/z +r)

calculate a true AABB for the sphere.

cone of length 1, radius r, in direction (x,y,z)
tilt plane y=0 about x until touches cone, project onto z=1 for AABB y limits. (1)
tilt plane x=0 about y until touches cone, project onto z=1 for AABB x limits. (2)

not hard. put a sphere in the cone like an ice cream.
tilt the cone, just consider where the sphere hits the tilted planes.

for a cone of height 1, radius of circle r, sphere positioned with centre at hypotenuese squared.
hyp*hyp = 1+ r*r = D.

and radius r * hyp = r * sqrt(1+ r*r) = R

for circle at position (x,y,z) the sphere has position (x,y,z)*D

for plane tilted about x axis, looking at projection onto yz plane.
the sphere here is seen as a circle of radius R at (y,z)*D

the range of angles from the origin pointing to the circle in this projection is: 

atan2(y,z) +/- asin( R / D ) 

can asin (R/D) be simplified? 
asin ( r * sqrt(1+ r*r) /(1+ r*r)) 

copilot tells me this simplifies to atan(r) !!

therefore suspect that range of angles = atan2(y,x) +/- atan(r)

the range of y when projected from origin onto z=1 is then 
  tan ( atan2(y,z) +/- atan(r) )

copilot says
tan ( atan2(y,z) + atan(r) )    =   (y + rz) / (z - ry)
tan ( atan2(y,z) - atan(r) )    =   (y - rz) / (z + ry)
